The journey

Seoul Wellness & Gastronomy

5 days / 4 nights · Private, for two

Day 1

Arrival & rest

  • VIP arrival assistance at Incheon
  • Private car to Four Seasons Hotel Seoul
  • Check-in and rest
  • Dinner at a Michelin-starred Korean fine-dining restaurant

Day 2

Spa, palace & craft

  • The Secret Garden of Changdeokgung
  • Lunch of Korean temple cuisine
  • Artisan craft: a private class making a mother-of-pearl hand mirror at a workshop in Insa-dong
  • Couple spa at Four Seasons in the late afternoon, 120 minutes
  • Dinner at a Michelin-starred modern Korean restaurant

Day 3

Hanok tea, Seoul Sky & K-beauty

  • Private tea ceremony at Rakkojae, a hanok in Bukchon
  • Lunch at a fine-dining restaurant on the 81st floor of Lotte World Tower
  • Seoul Sky observatory, floors 117–123 of Lotte World Tower
  • Private K-beauty session in Cheongdam: personal colour analysis and a one-to-one make-up lesson
  • Tea at the Lobby Lounge & Bar of the Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas, before dinner in the same hotel
  • Dinner at a Michelin-starred Japanese omakase counter

Day 4

A temple morning & the Han River

  • A guided walk through Bongeunsa, a thousand-year-old temple in Gangnam, then on foot to Starfield Library at COEX and coffee nearby
  • Lunch at a Michelin-starred Korean restaurant (if you choose the performance, lunch is at a Cantonese restaurant in a five-star hotel next to the theatre)
  • In the afternoon, your choice: a private yacht on the Han River, Leeum Museum of Art with a curator, or a traditional performance at the National Theater of Korea from VIP seats
  • Dinner: Hanwoo beef omakase in a private room at a MICHELIN Guide–level beef house, after a short walk through Majang meat market

Day 5

A palace morning & farewell

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace and the National Palace Museum, from the 10:00 guard ceremony (the museum only on Tuesdays, when the palace is closed)
  • Farewell lunch at a Michelin-starred modern Korean restaurant
  • VIP departure assistance

Before you send your request, you will see the full itinerary with approximate times.
